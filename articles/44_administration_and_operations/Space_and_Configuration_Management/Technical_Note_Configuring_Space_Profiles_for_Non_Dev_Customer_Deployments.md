# Configuring Space Profiles for K2cloud Non-Development Customer Deployments


## Abstract

The *Configuring Space Profiles for K2cloud Non-Development Customer Deployments* technical note describes how to tailor **Space Profiles** to meet the operational, performance, and reliability requirements of non-development environments (e.g., staging, production). It covers the information required from the customer (e.g., replication factor, disk/storage requirements), how resource allocations (CPU, memory) are determined, autoscaling settings, and how secrets/runtime parameters are configured. The goal is to ensure that Spaces are correctly sized and configured to avoid overcommitment, support high availability, and maintain predictable performance under load, rather than using default dev-oriented settings.
