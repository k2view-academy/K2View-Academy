# K2Cloud Space State Transition and Status (Technical Note)

## Abstract

The *K2Cloud Space State Transition and Status* technical note describes how K2view handles the lifecycle, state transitions, and status reporting of **Spaces** in the K2cloud platform. It explains the roles of major components — the K2Cloud Agent, Kubernetes, and the K2cloud Orchestrator — in determining and conveying the state of a Space (e.g., Creating, Running, Failed). The document outlines the process of state transitions, explains the meanings of status colors and indicators in the UI, describes the persistence of status, handles failures, and specifies the behaviors users can expect during deployment, termination, restart, and error conditions. It aims to help administrators understand what the system is doing under the hood, how to interpret status indicators, and how to troubleshoot when states don’t evolve as expected.
