# K2view Fabric Service Deployment Options

## Abstract

The K2view Fabric Service Deployment Options document outlines the flexible models available for deploying K2view Fabric and TDM services across cloud, on-premises, and hybrid environments. It explains how organizations can choose between fully SaaS-managed deployments, customer-hosted self-managed installations, or hybrid approaches that balance performance, compliance, and data locality. The guide details the roles of key components such as the K2cloud Orchestration Platform, Kubernetes clusters, and Git repositories, while clarifying the responsibilities shared between K2view and customers. Emphasis is placed on consistent operations, environment isolation, and secure connectivity, enabling enterprises to adopt the deployment strategy best suited to their business and regulatory needs.
