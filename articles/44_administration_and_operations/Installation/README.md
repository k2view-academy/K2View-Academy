<strong>Installation Options and Planning</strong>

This section holds a collection of documents related to deploying and installing K2view Fabric in various environments. It organizes guidance by category: system requirements, deployment models (SaaS, self-hosted, hybrid), cloud-provider-specific planning (AWS, Azure, GCP), and special installations such as air-gapped Kubernetes deployments. The purpose is to help users locate the appropriate detailed guide for their infrastructure, prerequisites, and security/compliance needs, ensuring smoother planning and execution of K2view installations.
