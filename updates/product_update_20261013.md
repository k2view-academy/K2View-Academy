### The Fabric 8.5.2 Official Release

We’re pleased to announce the release of K2view Fabric V8.5.2. 

This release makes AI in Studio more reliable and easier to control, makes Catalog discovery more flexible, and adds more operational control across the platform.

Refer to the [Release Notes](https://support.k2view.com/Academy/Release_Notes/V8.5/Fabric_Release_Notes_V8.5.2.pdf.html) for the full list of features and fixes.
