### The K2cloud November 2025 Release

We have released K2cloud November 2025 on November 2, 2025, at 11:00 UTC. 

For Project Managers, this update streamlines version governance and gives space owners one-click upgrades for both Studio and Fabric spaces.
* **Project-Level Version Promotion**: Project managers choose which Fabric image versions to expose per space profile. When a newer version is promoted on a Project’s Space Profile, space owners see it immediately and can upgrade. To enable a version, please open a ticket with the registry path(s) and tag(s) for the desired Fabric/Studio images so K2view can add them to your K2cloud configuration.
* **Self-Service Upgrades & Rollbacks**: Studio and Fabric spaces—including QA, Staging, and Production—receive an in-tile notification when project managers promote a new version. Owners can launch an upgrade/rollback with a guided confirmation and progress monitor.
* **New “View Space Details” Page**: A single place for a space summary and audit log, plus per-pod insights: image path, current phase, resource limits/requests, a downloadable raw pod description (for diagnostics), and the most recent 50 log lines.
* **Out-of-the-Box, Versionless Space Profiles**: Standard Small/Medium/Large configurations defined by CPU, memory, replicas, and storage type. View the list of [available profiles](https://support.k2view.com/Academy/articles/98_installation_and_upgrade/K2cloud_Space_Profiles/profiles.html).
  * **Existing customers**: Available on request—open a ticket and we’ll enable these profiles (and any desired versions) for your Studio, Staging, and Production environments. For new customers, these are provided by default during the provisioning process.


View the [release notes](https://support.k2view.com/Academy/Release_Notes/K2cloud/K2cloud_Release_Notes_Nov2025.pdf.html) and an [overview video](https://download.k2view.com/index.php/s/3rgZczMc6eEOU7t).
